Detective Achille

TypeScript · Three.js

A 1930s detective game in the manner of the golden age. Each case opens in M. Achille Verbeke’s rooms in London, where a client tells the story over sepia flashbacks. Then you visit the places as 3D dioramas, look closely, question everyone through Sergeant Hobbs, and fill the blanks of the detective’s deductions with the words you have noted. Seven cases, from a country house to a snowbound Alpine hotel, written and voiced in English, Turkish, French and Spanish, with a case designer for making new ones.

Tech Tycoon

TypeScript · Canvas

A tycoon game about AI and computing. From 2012 to 2035 you grow compute farms on an archipelago of the world's tech regions, design chips on real process nodes, and train, distil and release models whose quality follows the published scaling laws, racing parodies of the big labs and chipmakers. An almanac and a talking leather jacket explain the real technology as it comes up.

Astroseeker

Flutter · iOS

An astrology app for learning the subculture and for personal analysis. It computes natal charts on the device with a pure-Dart astronomy engine (VSOP87) and draws them over a real sky background. You pick a birthplace on a map, play time forward to watch the chart move, and can ask Gemini to interpret what you see.

Cloak of Death

Flutter · Unity

David Cockram's Atari text adventure, rebuilt twice. The Flutter edition draws the original room art from the cassette's bytecode and keeps the parser, the 27 rooms and the puzzle chain intact. The Unity edition turns the same rules into a first-person 3D house, where you carry, push, light and throw things instead of typing commands.

Caput

JavaScript · WebRTC

Small-sided online football in the spirit of ISS and Pro Evolution Soccer, from 1v1 up to 8v8 with AI players filling empty spots. Rooms run peer-to-peer in the browser with rollback netcode, so no server is needed. It plays with a keyboard, a gamepad or touch controls, and installs as an app.
